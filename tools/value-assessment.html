3Potential Economic Value (PEV) BA

Cashable vs non-cashable. Productivity value is non-cashable — real only if freed hours redeploy into revenue-driving work. 5Cost to deliver BA

Price AI like AI, not software: include tuning, retraining, data maintenance — and the risk of vendor price rises after 12 months invalidating the case.

Change budget check: 30–40% of AI budgets should go to adoption and change — value is created in the people-and-process 30%, but most investment goes the other way. Roll-out + education here = — of total cost.

8Assumption register BA

Every input above carries a RAG dot: green = data received · amber = assumption built for testing · red = needs data/validation. Validate key assumptions bottom-up during discovery; the POC should be instrumented to prove the amber ones.

9Measurement design & verdict DLBA

Design measurement into the build — the system logs its own evidence (cases, time, accuracy, escalations) so “does it pay?” is answered with data, not archaeology.
