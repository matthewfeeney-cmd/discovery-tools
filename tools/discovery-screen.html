How this works

Screen each candidate workflow in ~20 minutes with the person who knows it. Four Value questions, then twelve AI-readiness checks across four dimensions — fit · data · tech · skills — three answers each, pick the closest. If you can't answer, pick the left option: an unknown is a finding, and the score rises as you learn. You get scores, an indicative £ at stake, a build-vs-buy call and a ranked backlog — the shortlist that feeds Build. This is the light screen, not a full assessment: deep-dive the winners with the Value Assessment and Mini Op Model, and bring the top ones to life with quick prototypes on example cases.
