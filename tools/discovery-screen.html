How this works

Two layers. Organisation readiness (this tab, ~20 minutes, asked once with the sponsor and IT) sets the ceiling — how far this business can take any AI use case. Use cases (next tab) score the individual workflows within it. Readiness is a weakest-link system: your lowest dimension caps everything, so the score that matters is the ceiling, not the average.
