1Team readiness & skills

The organisation for a small business; the department or team for a large one. Scored once, ~30 minutes with the sponsor, IT and a team lead. This module stands alone — it's a deliverable in its own right. Readiness is a weakest-link system: the lowest dimension caps everything above it.

Interview log — who told us what

Over a two-week scan you'll speak to 12–20 people. Log each one: it's the audit trail behind every score, and the source list in the report.

NameRoleDateCohortWhat they told you that changed a score

Team skill scan — can these people work with AI?

Seven capabilities, scored across the team as a whole: the typical level people are at, and how widely it's held. Levels: 0 none · 1 aware · 2 with help · 3 independent · 4 can teach. Required levels rise with the 12-month target you set — so a Stage 4 ambition demands more than a Stage 2 one.

Cohort breakdown — who is short of what

A team-wide average hides the shape. Score each capability for the three cohorts separately — that's what turns a skills profile into a training plan someone can cost. Leave a cell blank where the capability isn't relevant to that cohort.

Codebase review — Overwatch engine, Ground Truth scoring

Run where the client has their own product or in-house development. One scan, two report angles: engineering quality and AI readiness of the codebase. The FDE owns it across both weeks. Skip it for a non-technical department — Technology is then scored by interview alone, and the report says so.
